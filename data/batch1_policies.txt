Own your home with a loan designed for salaried and self-employed families.

Key features

Resident Indian salaried employees with at least 2 years of work experience, and self-employed professionals and business owners with at least 3 years of business continuity.

Home loan protection insurance
Loan protection insurance from our bancassurance partners is available but is completely optional. Your loan sanction does not depend on buying any insurance product.

Visit any Sahyadri Union Bank branch or call our customer care number to apply. Terms and conditions apply.

SAHYADRI UNION BANK - RETAIL ASSETS DEPARTMENT
HOME LOAN PROCESSING GUIDELINES - VERSION 2
Effective from 1 April 2026. This version replaces Version 1 dated 1 July 2024.

1. Login
The branch must log in every home loan application in the Loan Origination System on the day the complete application is received. Use the standard document checklist. Incomplete files must not be logged.

2. Processing fee
Collect a processing fee of 0.35% of the loan amount plus GST, subject to a maximum of Rs. 10,000 plus GST, at the time of login. The fee is non-refundable once legal scrutiny has started.

3. Turnaround time
Sanction decision within 7 working days from receipt of the complete file. Branches expecting a delay must inform the Regional Retail Assets Hub in writing before the 7th working day.

4. Legal and valuation
- Legal opinion only from empanelled advocates.
- Valuation only from empanelled valuers.
- Properties valued above Rs. 1 crore require two independent valuations.

5. Insurance and cross-sell
Home loan protection insurance under the bancassurance arrangement is optional. Staff must not make insurance a condition for sanction or disbursement.

6. Sanctioning authority
- Branch Manager: up to Rs. 50 lakh
- Regional Head: above Rs. 50 lakh and up to Rs. 2 crore
- Zonal Credit Committee: above Rs. 2 crore

7. Disbursement
Disburse only after the mortgage is created, all conditions of sanction are met, and the disbursement checklist is signed by two officers.

The maximum tenure is 30 years, subject to the borrower being no older than 65 years at the end of the loan.

Concession limits and approval levels are internal. They must not be quoted to customers or dealers as a standing entitlement, and dealers must not be told the eligible concession range.

SAHYADRI UNION BANK - REGULAR SAVINGS ACCOUNT FAQ

Q1. Who can open a Regular Savings Account?
Any resident individual aged 18 years or above, and minors through a guardian, subject to KYC requirements.

Q2. What documents are needed?
Proof of identity and address as per KYC norms, a PAN card or Form 60, and a recent photograph. Aadhaar-based e-KYC is accepted.

Q3. What is the minimum balance to be maintained?
The Average Monthly Balance (AMB) required is Rs. 2,000 for branches in metro and urban areas, and Rs. 1,000 for branches in semi-urban and rural areas. A charge may apply if the AMB is not maintained, as per the bank's schedule of charges.

Q4. Is a zero-balance account available?
Yes. The Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account is available with no minimum balance requirement, with certain limits on services.

Q5. How is interest paid?
Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited to your account every quarter.

Q6. Do I get a debit card?
Yes. A RuPay debit card is issued free of cost. You get 5 free transactions per month at Sahyadri Union Bank ATMs.

Q7. Can I add a nominee?
Yes. You can add or change a nominee at any time by submitting the prescribed form at your branch.

For more details, visit any branch or contact customer care.

SAHYADRI UNION BANK - COMPLIANCE DEPARTMENT
KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER (KYC) POLICY - VERSION 2
Effective from 1 February 2026. This version replaces Version 1 dated 10 March 2024.

1. Customer identification
Every customer must be identified using officially valid documents or Aadhaar-based e-KYC before an account is opened or a loan is sanctioned. A PAN card or Form 60 is mandatory.

2. Risk categorisation
Customers are placed into three categories: Low, Medium and High risk. The rating of an individual customer is AML-Confidential and must never be disclosed to the customer or to any unauthorised staff member.

3. Periodic KYC updation
- High risk customers: every 1 year
- Medium risk customers: every 5 years
- Low risk customers: every 8 years

4. Enhanced due diligence (High risk)
- Record the source of funds and source of wealth.
- Branch Head approval is required to open or continue the account.
- Monitor transactions more frequently.

5. Politically exposed persons (PEPs)
Accounts of PEPs require prior approval of the Regional Compliance Office.

6. Record keeping
Keep KYC records for at least 5 years after the account is closed or the loan is fully repaid.

7. Staff responsibilities
Branch staff must not open accounts where identity cannot be verified. Any doubt about a customer's documents must be reported to the Branch AML Nodal Officer.

SAHYADRI UNION BANK - COMPLIANCE DEPARTMENT
ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML) TRANSACTION MONITORING POLICY - VERSION 2
Restricted to the AML and Compliance team. Effective from 15 May 2026. Replaces Version 1 dated 1 September 2024.

1. Alert scenarios
The monitoring system raises an alert when any of the following occurs:
- Cash deposits totalling Rs. 8 lakh or more in one account in a single day.
- Repeated cash deposits just below the alert level within 7 days (possible structuring).
- Rapid pass-through: 90% or more of a credit is debited within 48 hours.
- Sudden high activity in an account that has been dormant for 12 months or more.

2. Review timelines
- The Branch AML Nodal Officer must review each alert within 3 working days.
- Alerts that remain suspicious are escalated to the Principal Officer at the Regional Compliance Office.
- The decision on filing a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) must be taken within 7 working days of escalation.

3. No tipping off
No staff member may inform the customer, or anyone connected with the customer, that an alert has been raised, that an account is under review, or that an STR has been filed.

4. Risk rating changes
Any change in a customer's risk rating following an alert requires written approval from the Regional Compliance Office.

5. Access and records
Alert details, STR files and customer risk ratings are visible only to authorised AML and Compliance staff. Records are kept for 5 years.

SAHYADRI UNION BANK - RETAIL ASSETS DEPARTMENT
DIRECT SELLING AGENT (DSA) MANAGEMENT POLICY
For internal use. Payout rates and portfolio quality limits are confidential.

1. Empanelment
A DSA is empanelled for 2 years after due diligence, a signed agreement and a KYC check of the agency and its owners. Empanelment is reviewed every year.

2. Payout
- Home loans: 0.40% of the amount disbursed
- Auto loans: 0.60% of the amount disbursed
Payout is released within 15 days of full disbursement.

3. Clawback
If a loan sourced by a DSA turns 60 days or more past due within 6 months of disbursement, the full payout for that loan is recovered from the DSA's future payouts.

4. Quality monitoring
- A DSA whose early-stage overdue ratio exceeds 3% of loans sourced in a quarter is placed on the watchlist.
- Two consecutive quarters on the watchlist lead to suspension of new logins.

5. Conduct
DSAs must not collect any fee from customers, promise sanction dates or interest rates, or handle customer original documents. Any breach leads to blacklisting.

6. Branch responsibility
Branches must independently verify income and address details of every DSA-sourced application and must not rely on the DSA's verification.
