account: N/A
text:
Internal email. From: Anjali Bhagwat (Regional Compliance Office, Pune Region). To: All Branch Heads, Pune Region. Subject: KYC Policy Version 2 - periodic updation and PEP approvals.

Dear Branch Heads,

During recent branch reviews, we found that some branches are still applying the periodic KYC updation intervals from KYC Policy Version 1. Version 1 was valid only until 31 January 2026.

Please follow KYC Policy Version 2, effective from 1 February 2026:
- High risk customers: KYC updation every 1 year (earlier 2 years)
- Medium risk customers: every 5 years (earlier 8 years)
- Low risk customers: every 8 years (earlier 10 years)

Also note two other changes in Version 2:
1. Accounts of politically exposed persons (PEPs) now need prior approval from the Regional Compliance Office. Branch Head approval alone is no longer enough.
2. For high risk customers, the source of wealth must be recorded along with the source of funds.

Please remember that a customer's risk category must never be disclosed to the customer or to unauthorised staff, even if the customer asks directly. If asked, please say that the category is internal to the bank.

Please review your KYC due list and plan the pending updates in the coming weeks. Send any queries to the Regional Compliance Office.

Regards,
Anjali Bhagwat
Regional Compliance Office, Pune Region
